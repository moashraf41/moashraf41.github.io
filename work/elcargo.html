Project overview

Logistics planning platform where teams lay out a shipment in an interactive 3D scene: place containers, packages and pallets, stack them with real positioning, save the plan and export it as a PDF. Google sign-in, role-based admin for the cargo catalogue and a full Arabic/English interface.
