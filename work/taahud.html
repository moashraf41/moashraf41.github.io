Project overview

Saudi construction platform that connects contractors, engineering offices, real-estate developers and suppliers in one place: publish a project, receive quotes, sign digital contracts and release escrow payments as delivery milestones are met. It also ships Rasad, a project-intelligence layer that verifies project data and gives every project a unified reference number from licence to handover.
